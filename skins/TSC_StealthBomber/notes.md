# TSC_StealthBomber

The user's words (2026-10-07, Claude Opus 5.5): "do a stealth bomber", the first car made by the routine of a
visual language, then passes (.claude/skills/skin/language.md).

what makes stealth stealth: every edge aligned to the same sweep, and an edge that must turn breaking into sawtooth
teeth; two greys a step apart, tape over the seams, black only inside openings; the canopy's gold coating the only
accent; quiet, sparse, the detail in the edges.
