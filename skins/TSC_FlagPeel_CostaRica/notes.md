2026-09-26, Opus 5.5, on the Mac (painted in the container, checked in headless Chrome, shown in the
Lab's Studio). The user: "Design a skin for me. Give me another proposition for cmyk tearing
concept but in this case instead of cmyk, we could do country flags, for example Costa Rica.
What do you think?"

Read as: TSC_CMYK_Peel_More's matte black wrap torn open (the same tears: 45 %, scale 40,
seed 11), with a country's flag underneath instead of the CMYK run: a series, Costa Rica first
(another country is a line in `FLAGS` and a skin that loads this design). Costa Rica's stripes
(blue, white, red double, white, blue; the blue a touch lighter than the flag's navy so it shows
against the black) are draped over the car from side to side: red down the spine to the nose
tip, white over the shoulders and sidepods, blue down the sides. From the chase camera the whole
flag reads across the car in its order. Built from clay in six steps (the Studio's filmstrip).

- The rest in the flag's colours: the front wing carries the flag across it (blue tips, red
  centre), red glowing sidepod grilles, side vents, seat belts and cockpit rim, blue calipers
  and brake lights, white speed numbers, rear lights through the gears blue, blue, white,
  white, red, rims glowing red when braking hard, the inside's lamps in the drape's colours,
  the turbo red inside the wheels (exhaust heat, as on the CMYK car). Matte black wheel covers
  with a red line round each tyre; the tail matte black; quilted seat, heat-tinted exhaust.

  line now drops under the nose, so its tip is red all over. The front wing's paint had reached
  the wing pylons under the nose (the assembly): only the wing itself now. The mirrors in a
