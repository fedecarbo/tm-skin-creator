# TSC_Ensign

A test car of the tool's marks (shapes laid on a named panel), designed by an agent (Claude, Fable 5.1)
on 2026-10-05 at the user's request: "sure test car, send an agent?".

The concept: a racing livery in the language of a squadron's insignia, a car to drive rather than a
concept. Gloss navy body; the car's own panels in ivory (the nose panel, the sidepod tops, the rear
quarter panels, the tail panel), so every colour edge is one of the car's seams; a navy-ivory-red
roundel on each rear quarter panel (where the panel has most room) and a bigger one on the tail panel;
a red badge with an ivory star at the back of each sidepod top; an ivory star on the bonnet behind the
nose fin's plate; a red ring on the nose panel; an ivory number roundel on each rear flank's waist with
a row of three red mission marks above it; carbon skirts, diffuser and wing pylons; gunmetal wheels
with black hubs; the inner car dark grey; ivory speed numbers, red rear, brake and wheel lights.
Twelve marks in all; the badge, the bonnet star and the first mission mark are placed tight on
purpose, so the fitting has to move them.

- Tried and not kept (Claude): red wheel rings (louder than the insignia; gunmetal instead); three
  red dots trailing the flank roundel down towards the rear wheel (the third shrank to 1.9 cm in the
  wheel arch's roll, and all three sat in the tyre's shadow from most angles; a row above the roundel
  instead); a 24 cm nose ring on the 23 cm panel (fitted at 86 % it hugged the panel's edges; 17 cm
  now, with air round it); a gloss black sidepod frame (its paint also lands on the uprights and a
  quarter of the floor; one dark grey for the whole inner car instead).
- Shown (2026-10-05): in the Lab. Close looks checked: the bonnet star between the plate and the
  cockpit, the nose ring, the sidepod badge at the panel's back, the mission marks and the flank
  roundel, the tail roundel, the right side's twins, the night cameras (rear lights red, digits
  ivory). The checks name nothing.
- The user (2026-10-05), of the car in the Lab: "its a terrible design". A set of two takes was being
  painted for their eye (A the shapes laid on the panels, B the same shapes as placed, across whatever
  is there); dropped with the verdict. The tool's part on this car: all twelve shapes whole; the
  bonnet star, the sidepod badge and the first mission mark moved to stay off the nose fin's plate
  and the panels' edges.
