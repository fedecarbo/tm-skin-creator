## What it is
A car painted by the air: a dark test car wearing the bright streaks the wind leaves on it, the
way racing teams paint their cars with glowing flow paint to see where the air goes.

## Drawn from
- Flow-visualisation paint on Formula 1 test cars: a fluorescent yellow-green paint that the air
  drags into fine streaks as the car drives, showing the path of the air over the bodywork.
- Wind tunnels and airflow simulations: smoke lines, and the maps that colour the body where the
  air presses hardest (the nose, the front of the sidepods).
- The test car itself: raw matte black and carbon, sensor stickers and heat strips, a car still
  being worked on.

## Not
- Flames, swooshes or speed lines drawn for show: every streak follows the path the air really
  takes over the car.
- A gamer car with neon everywhere: the bright colour is only where the air puts it.
- A copy of any real team's car, livery or logos.
- The CMYK car again: no torn wrap, no colour run under a skin.
