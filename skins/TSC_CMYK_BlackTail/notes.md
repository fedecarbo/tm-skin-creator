Read as: "internal" is the inner car (the user's word for it since TSC_Stealth_CMYK): finish
every inside part, seen or not, and build what the tool lacked for it (relief, on the
improvement list). Then the user's changes from the game. This take is the user's idea for the
tail; TSC_CMYK_EndsInK is Claude's. Both carry everything else.

Both takes, against TSC_CMYK_Peel_More:
- The inside, checked part by part (a picture of each of the 58 inner parts on its own): every
  light the game put there and the design never touched was still stock teal or white (lamps in
  the cockpit tub, bulkhead and nose, the light strip under the floor, the front wing's end
  strips, the vanes, the faint night glows). They take the body's run where they sit (cyan at
  the nose, magenta in the middle, orange at the tail), each keeping its brightness; the hubs'
  lamps cyan. The exhaust (bare titanium, the one bright silver part) is heat-tinted, gold to
  purple to blue; the openings under the quarter panels (seen when the air brakes lift), still
  stock grey, are dark.
- Relief, new in the paint box: a quilted seat (7 cm diamonds), and a printer's registration
  mark raised at each end of the tail's top band.
- The turbo: the game lit the whole inside of each wheel (and the suspension, the fins in the
  tail) in the pad's colour, yellow after a yellow pad; the user didn't like it and prefers
  magenta. The turbo colour is the game's, so those parts now carry exhaust heat, which keeps
  its own colour: magenta in the wheels, the run's colour elsewhere, and orange-hot inside the
  tail's two openings. Exhaust heat has never been seen to light in the game: this is its test.
  If it doesn't light, the wheels simply stay dark in a turbo.
- The wheels: one line per tyre. The glowing ring inside each wheel is dark; the tyre's line is
  1.6 cm (31.5 to 33.1 cm from the axle) instead of 1.2.
- The speed digits orange, the run's end colour (were magenta).
- The tail (rear diffuser, rear bumper, rear strakes) matte black like the wrap, not orange.

This take: the wrap holds over the tail's last 40 cm (the body's tears shrink away towards the
tip), and one bold tear across the tail's top band shows the orange under it.
