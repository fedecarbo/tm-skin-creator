# TSC_Stealth_CMYK

2026-09-24. Grown from TSC_Seams_Black (see its notes). The user: "any ideas on how we can paint the inner mechanisms and cables?" They liked the accent approach and the glow approach, then asked: "What do you think about a CMYK sort of gradient? I know it's not a stealth thing, but I think it would be nice for internal. If not, ice blue or a magenta colour."

Version 1: the stealth black body; inside, raw materials (carbon arms, gunmetal dampers/hubs/sidepod frames, titanium exhaust, black leather); the accent parts (sidepod grilles, side vents, calipers, belts, brake lines) run cyan > magenta > yellow across each grille and along the belts, glowing; calipers cyan (all four share one patch of paint, so they can't fade front to back) and lit when braking; vents yellow. A first try lit the whole sidepod frame: a glowing slab, dropped. Candidates alongside: TSC_Stealth_Magenta, TSC_Stealth_Ice.

Version 2 (2026-09-24), the user: not sure about "the blue metallic colour" (the gunmetal), "should we keep it just black?"; likes the CMYK but "there's not much you can actually see". Now: the metal parts and rims are satin black; the run goes over the whole sidepod (frame, grille, outer panel) along the car, cyan at the front to yellow at the back, plus the belts; front wing endplates cyan, rear strakes yellow. TSC_Stealth_CMYK_Bold pushes it further.
