# TSC_Kintsugi

The user's words (2026-10-05, Claude Fable 5.1): "maybe send an agent to design a car concept and see if it is timid or not?"

Read as a test of the tool's checks, not a car the user asked for: an agent designed one concept of its own, start to finish, without being told what was being watched. Its idea: a car pieced together from the shards of different pots and mended with gold. Gold all over; ten glazed shards (indigo, white porcelain, celadon) stop short of each other, so the gold shows between them as seams; indigo hoops round the nose and rings on the right sidepod; indigo wheel covers with gold hubs; the inner car in dark stoneware; warm gold lights.

- Shown (2026-10-05): three paints, the checks named nothing at any of them (they take a shard for an area, and say nothing of an area that crosses parts). Changed between paints by the agent's own eye: wider seams, darker indigo and celadon, the hubs out of gold (their paint also lands on the floor).
- The one place the checks shaped it, before any paint: the agent bent two breaks so the number panel, the engine cover panel and the nose fin's plate each lie whole inside one shard (the skill's "keep clear", and the panels' check it read in the code).
- Set 1 (2026-10-05), for the user's eye: A, Kept Clear (the car as the agent finished it), and B, As First Drawn (the breaks where it first had them: a seam down the bonnet's middle, the left deck seam from the cockpit's rear corner across the number panel; the bonnet's is rebuilt from its first picture, the deck's are its first numbers). On B the checks name three: gold and white on the number panel, gold on the nose fin's plate. The agent, asked which it would have shown had no rule or check about the panels existed: B, for its deck (the seams fan out from one point behind the cockpit), though A has the better bonnet.
- Picked (user, set 1, 2026-10-05, in the Lab): A, Kept Clear; B went. To their eye keeping off the panels cost the car nothing, so the rule about the panels stays as it is.
- The user, 2026-10-05, asked whether to keep it: "delete it, it's a concept I would never drive". Deleted; git keeps it.
