## What it is
A bright bug in the grass, made for the grass maps and fun to drive there: the top of the car
stands out against the green like a ladybird on a leaf, and the bottom rises out of a fringe of
grass that blends it into the map.

## Drawn from
- Ladybirds, bees and beetles: the colours nature uses to say "look at me" against green.
- A jockey's racing colours: bold shapes in two strong colours, made to be picked out at a gallop
  across the turf.
- The lawn seen at ground level: blades of grass drawn as a clean, graphic edge, like an
  illustration, not a photo.

## Not
- Camouflage, or green all over.
- Grass as a photo, or grass anywhere but the bottom.
- A toy or a cartoon bug: no eyes, antennae or face.

## Fixed
- A fringe of grass along the bottom, the blend between the map and the car.
