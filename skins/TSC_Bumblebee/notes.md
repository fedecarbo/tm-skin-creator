# TSC_Bumblebee

The user's words (2026-10-09, Claude Fable 5.1): "Bumblebee", picked from six ideas offered in the Lab's chat
(the idea as offered: "fuzzy matte yellow and black, a worker's body"); an earlier pick, Chalk hull, they didn't

How I read them: the insect, not a cartoon or a hornet: a fat, furry worker in velvet black and ochre yellow,
a white tail, pollen in its fur, hard shiny black legs. Built by the routine: the language first, then the
composition in three takes, then surfaces, details and art direction.

- Language written and shown 2026-10-09 (its page in the Lab); OK (user, 2026-10-09, in the Lab): "yes".
- Set 1, Composition · 3 takes, opened 2026-10-09: A True bee (the bee's own banding along the car: black nose, a yellow
  collar behind the nose, black over the cockpit, yellow over the sidepods, black deck, white tail), B Four bands (black
  nose, yellow from the nose root over the cockpit, black over the sidepods and deck, white tail), C Chevron (the bands'

  What each big shape is for: the black nose is the head; the yellow collar (z 110 to 150) is the front of the thorax;

- Surfaces pass (2026-10-09, Claude Fable 5.1): the fur's lay, a thin grain along the car over the suede in each
  band's own colour (suede alone reads as a cloudy mottle with no direction; the grain gives the coat a nap that
  lies nose to tail and catches the light along it), the coat pressed to a soft velvet sheen where it brushes the

  the legs in honey amber, the wings' colour lit, by day and at night (the speed digits, the rear lights, the brake

  line; the tail's fur over the diffuser's outline) are the fur crossing the car's lines on purpose.
