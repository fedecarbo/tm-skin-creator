# Streamliner: the brief

The user's words (2026-09-29): "I want you to come up with a concept to build a car"

## What it is
A car off the drawing boards of the 1930s streamlined record breakers: deep glossy blue, fine
lining, polished steel speed lines and red wheels, as if the fastest locomotive of 1938 had been
redrawn for the stadium.

## Drawn from
- The streamlined express locomotives of the late 1930s (the Mallard, 203 km/h in 1938): the deep
  garter blue, the wedge nose, the red wheels, the thin lining along every edge.
- A.M. Cassandre's Art Deco travel posters (Nord Express, 1927): speed drawn as long converging
  lines, big simple shapes, no clutter.
- Polished stainless steel Art Deco trim (the Chrysler Building's crown, a 1930s diner): bright
  metal bands that catch the light.

## Not
- A toy train: no face, funnel or cartoon steam.
- Steampunk: no brass, gears or rivets.
- A museum piece: nothing aged, sepia or worn. It looks brand new and fast.
- A poster pasted on a car: the lines follow the car's own shape.
