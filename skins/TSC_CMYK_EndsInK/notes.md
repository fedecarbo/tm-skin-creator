# TSC_CMYK_EndsInK

2026-09-25, Opus 5.5. Claude's idea next to the user's (TSC_CMYK_BlackTail: the user's words and
everything both takes share are in its notes). The user: "but you could provide your concept
without my asking, so we have maybe you approach and mine? and see what we pick".

The idea: CMYK ends in K. The colour under the wrap runs cyan, magenta, orange and then black at
the very tip (matte, like the wrap), so the tears fade into the black towards the tail, which is
clean black. The orange comes back only as light: the speed digits, the rear lights' last band,
the side vents seen through the tail's openings, and those openings in a turbo. A first try had
the tip's black satin: under the matte wrap it caught the light as pale grey blotches, like dirt.
