# TSC_WearTest

The road's step 11 (IMPROVEMENTS.md, 2026-10-09, Claude Opus 5.5): a test car for wear, scrapped after the pick. The
same design painted twice: A with the tool as it was (the code at 0b467fe: chips where a face turns forward, low on
the sides and on the nose; fading and the clear coat's failing on faces turned up and high; scrapes in a band 20 to
55 cm up on the outermost sides), B read off the car's own shape (the wear maps baked once per car: chips along its
outward edges, corners, shoulders and lips, more on thin blades, none in a crevice; fading and the clear coat's
failing under the open sky; scrapes on the body's outline seen from above; grime where the air doesn't reach and
down the grooves). The same calls but two: A chips 0.04 of the whole body (its meaning), B 0.4 of its edges, which
is 4 % of the body, the same paint lost; A has no grime (it had none).

- Set 1 (Wear · 2 takes): A as it was, B on the wear maps.
- Shown (2026-10-09, Claude Opus 5.5): set 1 open in the Lab, A (TSC_WearTest_AsItWas, painted from the old code's
  tree, 0b467fe) and B (TSC_WearTest_WhereCarsWear). The livery first had a white stripe down the middle; the judge
  blocked it (two paints on the deck's lettered panels), so both takes are blue over red. The tool's notes: A "faded
  up to 25%, 8% clear coat gone, 0.7% scraped, 4.0% chipped"; B "8% clear coat gone, 0.3% scraped, chipped: 36% of
  the edges, 0.7% of the open panels, grime on 24%". Close up: A's chips a spray on the faces turned forward and low
  (the nose, the sidepods' front corner, the low sides), its clear coat gone in big patches on top; B's along the
  nose's edges, the cockpit's rim, the sidepods' shoulders, the rear shoulders and the bottom edge, a light scatter
  elsewhere, the grime under the car, inside the inlets and down the panel lines.
- Pick of set 1 (user, 2026-10-09, in the Lab): B, "Where cars wear": the OK for the road's step 11. The takes gone
