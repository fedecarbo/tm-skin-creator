# TSC_CMYK_Halftone

2026-09-27, Opus 5.5. The user, after installing TSC_CMYK_EndsInK: "Yes.  Can we start adding
some texture, more like for the black paint?"

Read as: a texture in the black wrap only (the colour under the tears stays as it is). "Texture"
is open, so three readings in a round ("Texture in the black"): A carbon fibre (a material),
B a halftone print (a pattern, from the CMYK idea), C brushed black metal (a sheen). This is take
B, a halftone print. It is TSC_CMYK_EndsInK with its wrap painted before the tears are cut (the
wrap hook in TSC_CMYK_Peel's design), so the tears stay exactly where they are.

Take B: CMYK is print, so the wrap is printed black on black: a halftone screen of glossy dots (1.8
cm apart) on the matte black, from 30 % of the spacing at the nose to 90 % at the tail. A dot that
would bend over a fold, seam or tight curve is left out whole: first cut by the seams, then the
sidepod inlet's lip still showed a row of bright dashes (the highlight through the dots) until the
rule was 14 degrees.
