# Water: the brief

The user's words (2026-10-01): "I want to build a car that is perfect for water maps.  In this case
I want to first paint the car with layers of cyan all over the place.  That's going to be the first
things to do, will tell you what to do after that"

## What it is
A car for the water maps whose whole body is built from layers of cyan, from pale shallows to deep
water: it belongs on the water and still stands out from it.

## Drawn from
- Water seen from above, shallows over sand down to the deep channel: the cyans and the way they
  step from one to the next.
- Offshore powerboat racing: wet gloss, shapes made to be seen at speed over water.
- The water maps themselves: deep blue water and white spray, which the car is seen against.

## Not
- One flat cyan: the layers are the idea, so each step must be clearly its own.
- Lost against the water: light against dark between the layers, so the car reads over deep blue.
- An ice car: pale cyan and white together read as ice maps, not water.
- Cartoon water: no clip-art splashes, drops, bubbles or fish.
- A gamer RGB car: no glowing neon edges.

## Fixed
- Made for the water maps.
- Layers of cyan all over the car, first. The user directs what comes after.
