## 1. The professional livery design process (brief, visual language, whole-car composition, templates, details)

### Takeaway
Professionals run a funnel: an interview-style brief (including what the client does *not* want), a team "design language/DNA", many rough whole-car directions narrowed in rounds (Mercedes: about 60 to 30 to 10 to 1; Blackmore: three rounds), then a move into 3D/CAD to see how the graphics read on the real curvature, and only at the end sponsor balancing and detail. They design for how the car is seen (TV camera, the car rotating past the camera), use the body's contours for direction, keep negative space, and keep logos/numbers on clear zones.

### Cited Findings
- Andy Blackmore (livery designer with a car-design background) starts by getting inside the client's head; "Not everyone has a brief, so that can also be a challenge in itself." He establishes first what the team does *not* want, then sponsor requirements and team identity. — [Jalopnik, "How a race car gets its paint job" (2016-01-19)](https://www.jalopnik.com/how-a-race-car-gets-its-paint-job-1752512826/)
- The interview goes beyond the car: designers ask what music the client likes, favourite painters, hobbies (snippet). — [Motor Authority, "Designing a race car livery is like walking on a tightrope"](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1125360_designing-a-race-car-livery-is-like-walking-on-a-tightrope)
- Blackmore's rounds: round 1 rough concept sketches exploring different directions; round 2 develops the chosen theme with detail variations; round 3 final balancing and sponsor placement. Rush jobs take 4-5 days, otherwise weeks. — [Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/how-a-race-car-gets-its-paint-job-1752512826/)
- Designers need line art (templates) of the car; Blackmore gets it from manufacturers or reconstructs it from long-lens photos and measurements. — [Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/how-a-race-car-gets-its-paint-job-1752512826/)
- 2D-to-3D is the hard part: on curved cars (his example, a Porsche Cayman) graphics that look right flat distort on the body; he validated the Cayman design by placing printed mock-ups on a showroom car. — [Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/how-a-race-car-gets-its-paint-job-1752512826/)
- Blackmore's rules of thumb: convey speed with angled elements that follow the car's contours; use negative space deliberately, especially on premium cars; "For me, clarity of partner logos is really important"; balance logos without overcrowding; front positions preferred over rear; the livery should suit the car ("your first thought wouldn't be to do a drift-style livery on a Bentley Continental"). — [Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/how-a-race-car-gets-its-paint-job-1752512826/)
- Clean, elegant liveries often use the car's body lines, while more graphical/abstract ones may not; designers work from 3D renderings because reflections, panel changes and complex shapes alter how lines and colour blocks read (snippet). — [Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1125360_designing-a-race-car-livery-is-like-walking-on-a-tightrope)
- Mercedes-AMG F1: the livery takes over seven months (July to December), with about "60 design proposals, which then reduce to 30, 10 and the final one"; "The fading is unique to our design ... inspired by the core DNA of our design language"; the W09 livery emphasised "every single line for better visibility on camera"; "shape and proportion" named as the biggest challenges. — [Mercedes-AMG F1, "Insight: How to design an F1 car livery"](https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/news/insight-how-to-design-an-f1-car-livery)
- Alpine F1 (Sean Bull): brand link first ("a metallic blue similar to the classic A110 ... the most obviously identifiable link"); the "A" graphic placed on the rear side panel, keeping other areas clean for sponsors; "Once we have a base design sorted, we move this into CAD to see how the elements work on 3D objects"; designed for the camera: "clean blue from the front on or raised profile, then the evolution of a more detailed and intricate design as the car rotates around the camera." — [Motor Sport Magazine (2021-04-09)](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/f1/how-alpine-designed-its-striking-new-f1-livery/)
- Spindrift Media (livery studio): designs on "bespoke 5-view vector templates" (left, right, front, rear, top) with "geometric distortion correction"; "While 3D renders offer a stylised view for presentations, 2D vector blueprint renderings are the only format that allows for the mathematical precision required by professional paint and wrap teams"; sponsor hierarchy set by identifying the chassis' primary "TV Zones". — [Spindrift Media, race car livery design guide](https://www.spindriftmedia.com/insights/race-car-livery-design-guide)
- Popin Graphics (Nov 2025): "Car type and geometry - curves, vents, and bodylines affect layout"; "Directional graphics emphasise the car's aerodynamic shape, guiding the eye from front to rear"; high contrast keeps logos and numbers crisp; "every visual touchpoint should reflect a single coherent design language." — [Popin Graphics, complete guide to motorsport livery design (2025-11-12)](https://www.popingraphics.com/motorsport-livery/the-complete-guide-to-motorsport-livery-design-and-race-car-wraps)
- Wrap installers advise involving the installer on new vehicle types to know "the body lines, the curves, the dimensions, and the panels" so the design avoids alignment or overstretching problems; templates carry angular distortion, so study the car's lines in person (snippet). — [signshop.com](https://www.signshop.com/?p=22854); [signs101, 3M "Five tips for designing a great vehicle wrap"](https://www.signs101.com/threads/five-tips-for-designing-a-great-vehicle-wrap.89714)

### Inferences
- The pro order is: identity and "don't wants" -> design language -> several rough whole-car directions -> pick -> verify on 3D -> balance -> details. Details and logos are explicitly last; nobody composes part by part.
- Two different "follow the body" doctrines co-exist: clean liveries use body lines; graphic/abstract ones deliberately ignore them. Whether to follow body lines is a decision of the concept, not a universal rule.
- Pros design for a viewing condition (TV camera, the car rotating past it). The equivalent in Trackmania is the chase camera (rear three-quarter, slightly above), plus the garage/menu view.
- Pros use 3D for judging and 2D/vector templates for precision; neither alone is trusted.

- No quantitative rule (e.g., minimum number height for readability at a given distance) was found in the sources fetched.

## 2. Tools pros and hobbyists use in 2026 (Illustrator/Photoshop, Substance 3D Painter, Blender, wrap software) and recent AI features

### Takeaway
The 2D side is still vector templates (Illustrator) plus Photoshop; the 3D side is Substance 3D Painter, whose path tools gained a "Filled path" (11.0, 2025) and a "Ribbon" path (11.1, November 2025) that repeats or stretches a texture or text along a path drawn on the 3D surface without cuts, crossing gaps and object boundaries. Blender with DECALmachine projects mesh decals on curved surfaces. Painter is available as a $199.99 one-time Steam licence (no generative AI in that edition) or by subscription.

### Cited Findings
- Substance 3D Painter 11.1 (released 2025-11-18) adds the Ribbon path tool: "a tool that allows to repeat or stretch a texture along a path"; "Font resources can be used directly on a Ribbon path. Text automatically adjusts to the path to deform along it curves"; start/end sections controlled by "stretch between offsets"; works with mirror and radial symmetry; 75 presets including hard-surface panel lines and tapes. Fill layers and effects also gained symmetry with 3D projection modes, with optional axis flipping so mirrored logos stay readable. No AI features in this release. — [Adobe, Substance 3D Painter 11.1 release notes](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/release-notes/version-11-1)
- The Ribbon "will transform and repeat a texture along a path without any cuts", enabling text along paths, perfect gradients along a path, and trims that wrap around a mesh (snippet). — [Adobe 11.1 release notes](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/release-notes/version-11-1)
- Painter 11.0's Filled path tool creates shapes on the 3D surface filled with a uniform colour; it "can fit any kind of surfaces and is not restricted to planar areas, as it can cross gaps and object boundaries" (snippet; the search summary dated 11.0 to March 2025, consistent with a GDC 2025 article on a Painter feature). — [Adobe, Painter 11.0 release notes](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/release-notes/version-11-0); [Creative Bloq, GDC 2025](https://creativebloq.com/3d/gdc-2025-adobe-substance-3d-painter-gets-a-small-but-important-feature)

### Inferences
- The industry's answer to "tapes with gaps and steps" is path-based painting on the 3D surface: a path drawn once in 3D, with a texture or stroke laid along it, independent of UV islands and object boundaries. Painter's Ribbon (Nov 2025) is the current reference implementation, and text on a curve is a special case of it.
- Symmetry with readable (un-mirrored) text is now a standard expectation in pro tools.
- For this project, Painter's $199.99 one-time Steam licence fits the "one-time payment" preference, but Painter is an interactive GUI tool; using it as an engine for an AI-driven pipeline was not researched (Painter has a scripting API, but I did not verify its reach in this session).

## 3. Game livery editors: how decals and stripes behave on curved bodies, and which UX patterns help non-experts

### Takeaway
Every mainstream editor projects flat shapes onto the 3D body, and every one shows the same failure: steps and stretching where a graphic crosses from one projection side or panel to another. They manage it with a choice of projection mode (GT7: "aligned with surface" vs "aligned with camera", plus angle and depth limits on how far a decal wraps), symmetry/mirror to the other side, masks, groups, building logos large in a flat grid editor, highlighting paintable areas, and live preview in the sim (Trading Paints Sim Preview). Newer editors (F1 25, FH6) moved toward free placement of stickers on the surface instead of fixed slots.

### Cited Findings
- GT7 projection modes: "Aligned with surface": "the sticker keeps its original proportions and is rendered as if it was physically applied and bent over the surface"; "Aligned with camera": "will twist the proportions of the sticker so it appears unchanged to the eye from the camera angle defined at the moment of application"; "angle and depth parameters are also important to play with to either extend or restrict how much the sticker can wrap around elements." Stickers can be "copied on the opposite side or mirrored", masked, reordered; numeric input for angles and sizes; R3 highlights each editable area in cyan. — [Coach Dave Academy, how to use the GT7 livery editor (2025-02-11)](https://coachdaveacademy.com/tutorials/how-to-use-the-gt7-livery-editor/)
- GT7 Editor Options set the default projection method for decals (snippet). — [GT7 online manual, livery editor](https://gran-turismo.com/us/gt7/manual/liveryeditor/01)
- GT7 SVG decal upload: SVG, max 15 KB, uploaded through the Gran Turismo website. — [racinggames.gg (2022-03-21)](https://racinggames.gg/article/how-to-upload-decals-to-gran-turismo-7). SVG must be version 1.0/1.1, gradients may not work, and text must be converted to paths (snippet). — [racinggames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/gran-turismo/how-to-upload-decals-to-gran-turismo-7/)
- GT7 update 1.54 added preset brand decals (Brembo, Rotiform) and removed duplicate shapes (no projection changes found). — [racinggames.gg, GT7 1.54 patch notes](https://racinggames.gg/article/gran-turismo-7-update-154-patch-notes)
- Forza vinyl groups: build every logo in the flat vinyl-group editor with a grid; work large ("The bigger you make it to start, the harder it is to see minor imperfections once you size it down"); group letters and logos; mask unwanted edges with shapes in the base colour; build complex shapes by stacking and skewing primitives (snippets). — [Forza forums, FH3 vinyl group tips](https://forums.forza.net/t/are-there-any-tips-and-tricks-threads-for-creating-vinyl-groups-in-horizon-3/44990); [Forza forums, Masking HELP](https://forums.forza.net/t/masking-help/96248)
- In an early Forza editor, a top-surface vinyl needed an offset to centre, mirrored vinyls needed hand-adjusted height, and "the rear panel does not allow for a vinyl shape to be extended onto either of the side panels exactly without a step that is impossible to align perfectly" (snippet; old thread, Forza 2 era). — [GTPlanet, "Vinyl editing - how does it work?"](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/vinyl-editing-how-does-it-work.95603/post-2688724)
- Forza Horizon 6 (2026): liveries and decals can now go on windows and windshields (sun strips, stickers), more parts are paintable individually (wing struts, end plates, rims, mirrors), new finishes (candy, chameleon, enhanced chrome), saved favourite colours, vinyls imported from earlier Forza games. — [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/forza-horizon-6s-car-customization-is-getting-a-welcome-overhaul.md); [u4n, FH6 livery editor what's new](https://www.u4n.com/news/forza-horizon-6-livery-editor-whats-new.html)
- F1 25 (2025): new Decal Editor; liveries are a base pattern plus sponsor stickers that can be moved, scaled and rotated "on the surface of the car", replacing fixed sponsor slots; sponsor liveries are built around one title sponsor as the core of the design. — [EA, F1 25 customisation](https://www.ea.com/games/f1/f1-25/features/customisation); [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/f1-25-revamps-customization-new-decal-editor-driver-numbers)
- iRacing / Trading Paints Paint Builder (browser editor, launched 2023-06-07): 2048x2048 paints, 400+ vector graphics, fictional-brand logos, PNG/JPG/SVG import, lines/polygons/freehand, skew, shadow, blend modes, iRacing base-paint patterns, real-time collaboration; for Trading Paints Pro members (subscription). — [Trading Paints blog](https://blog.tradingpaints.com/introducing-the-new-paint-builder-our-biggest-update-yet/)
- Paint Builder "Sim Preview" pushes the project into iRacing's own 3D car viewer and updates live as you edit; January 2026 update shows your typed text in the font menu. — [Trading Paints help, Sim Preview](https://help.tradingpaints.com/paint-builder/sim-preview-viewing-your-paint-builder-projects-in-iracings-3d-car-model-viewer/); [Trading Paints, 2026-01-09](https://blog.tradingpaints.com/this-week-on-trading-paints-january-9-2026/)
- Assetto Corsa Competizione separates the livery into layers that each carry a material: decals.png (max 2048) and sponsors.png (max 4096), each with a JSON of roughness/clear coat/metallic (e.g., matte: baseRoughness 0.45, clearCoat 0.3); advice to keep colours within 10/10/10 and 237/237/237 RGB. — [Bsimracing, ACC custom livery tutorial (2020-05-24)](https://bsimracing.com/?p=80816)
- Assetto Corsa EVO 0.8 (2025) added support for externally made liveries; no in-game decal editor documented. — [Bsimracing, ACE 0.8](https://www.bsimracing.com/assetto-corsa-evo-version-0-8-released/)

### Inferences
- The step at a panel or projection boundary is universal and structural in projection-based editors; it is not a bug of one tool. Editors expose controls (projection mode, wrap angle/depth limit, masks) so the user decides where a graphic stops, instead of trying to make every crossing perfect.
- The UX patterns that let non-experts succeed: (a) start from a base pattern and add a few movable elements (F1 25, Paint Builder base paints); (b) symmetry/mirror with readable text; (c) layers, groups and masks; (d) a flat grid editor for logos and letters, placed later as one unit; (e) highlight what is paintable; (f) preview in the game's own renderer, live.
- ACC's split of "graphics" and "sponsors" into separate layers, each with one finish, is a simple model for a brand book: each layer of the design has one material.

## 4. The Trackmania 2020 skin community: how CarSport skins are made, file requirements, gotchas

### Takeaway
Nadeo's only official resource is the August 2020 Stadium car pack (UV maps per texture set, the 3D car model, baked maps; DDS in BC1/BC3/BC4/BC5). Serious creators use Substance Painter (texturing) and Blender (renders), sell premium skins on Gumroad, and avoid the in-game editor, which players call clunky and unable to touch lights, tyres or details. Installing needs Club access and a zip of DDS textures plus Icon.tga in Documents\Trackmania\Skins\Models\CarSport. A new open-source browser editor (three.js, DDS encoding in JS) appeared in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Nadeo's "Stadium CAR ressources" post (Ubi-Alinoa, 2020-08-06) provides "UV maps for each set of texture, as well as the 3D model of the car and some baked maps in bonus". Texture sets and compressions: Skin = base colour (BC1), roughness/metalness (BC5), varnish/clear coat (BC4), dirt mask (BC4); Details = base colour (BC1), roughness/metalness (BC5), self-illumination (BC3), normal (BC5), dirt mask (BC4); Wheels = base colour (BC1), roughness/metalness (BC5), normal (BC5), dirt mask (BC4); Glass = base colour (BC1), self-illumination (BC5). "Zip the textures together along with an Icon.tga file" and place the zip in Documents\Trackmania\Skins\Models\CarSport. Not allowed: custom 3D models, normal maps on the Skin textures, or changing gameplay displays on the car. — [devtrackers.gg mirror of Nadeo's post](https://devtrackers.gg/trackmania/p/3490d99c-stadium-car-ressources-all-you-need-to-create-skins-for-the-stadium-cars)
- In Skin_R the red channel is roughness and green is metallic; other editable maps named Skin_CoatR (varnish), Skin_DirtMask, Details_I (self-illumination) (snippet; the official texture-list page fetched did not contain car textures). — [doc.trackmania.com, texture list](https://doc.trackmania.com/create/texture-mods/texture-list/)
- BC1/DXT1 stores 5 bits red, 6 green, 5 blue per colour (relevant to banding in gradients on Skin_B). — [Nexus Mods forum, BC1/BC5/BC7 tutorial (snippet)](https://forums.nexusmods.com/topic/6934126-tutorial-how-to-importexport-bm-nm-bc1-bc5u-bc7-textures)
- Club access is required to make and use custom skins; upload path is Create > Garage > Upload Skin. — [Steam guide, "Uploading custom skins to Trackmania" (2023-02-02)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2927492235)
- Club "Skin uploads" activity shares skins with the public or club members; "Uploading Car Skins to the Skin Upload activity can only be done on the PC version". No size limit documented. — [doc.trackmania.com, skin uploads](https://doc.trackmania.com/club/activities/skin-uploads/)
- The in-game skin editor exists (stickers, layers), but a player in July 2025: "the in-game editor is not only clunky but you straight up can't work with lights/tires/details and much more in there", recommending Substance Painter, Photoshop or GIMP; the thread also reports saved skins not appearing in the selection menu. — [Steam discussion, "Skin editor is strange" (2025-07-09)](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2225070/discussions/0/592902205937371450/)
- TM Skin Studio (GitHub RobertKaa/skion-studio-tm20): browser editor for CarSport, TypeScript + Vite + three.js; edits six textures (body, roughness/metal, varnish, dirt mask, details, wheels) with brushes, shapes, text, image import, layers with masks/locks/opacity, an overlaid UV guide, undo/redo; encodes DDS in JavaScript with full mipmap chains and exports a zip with an Icon.tga. Its 3D model is a simplified non-Nadeo model, so the preview's UVs are "approximate rather than pixel-perfect"; BC1 encoding favours speed over quality; very new (5 commits). — [GitHub, skion-studio-tm20](https://github.com/RobertKaa/skion-studio-tm20)
- Premium and free TM skins are sold through Gumroad stores (e.g., TM Skins, Norglace, Bassjumper); skin zips from major creators range about 3.5 MB to 21.6 MB (search-summary figure, unverified). — [tmskins.gumroad.com](https://tmskins.gumroad.com/); [norglace.gumroad.com](https://norglace.gumroad.com/l/ynTDs)
- Openplanet plugins around cars are about skids (Modless Skids, skid managers) and vehicle unlock, not skin authoring; no Openplanet skin-preview/editor plugin was found. — [Openplanet, Modless Skids](https://openplanet.dev/plugin/modless-skids); [Openplanet, Vehicle unlock](https://openplanet.dev/plugin/vehicleunlock)

## 5. Lettering and type on curved car bodies

### Takeaway
Pros keep text off compound curves: they put lettering and logos on the flattest panels (doors, flat side zones, rear wing end plates), treat logos as separate overlays that can be moved to flat areas, use colour patterns rather than text where the body curves, and keep text as vector art. When text must follow a curve, 3D tools now deform it along a path drawn on the surface (Painter Ribbon), and projection editors let you choose surface-aligned vs camera-aligned projection.

### Cited Findings
- "Curves will distort the vinyl, so it's good to leave text away from these areas and use a pattern of color in these areas instead"; logos and graphics "can be printed as overlays, allowing you to move the logos or graphics to flat parts of the vehicle easily" (snippet, 3M tips). — [signs101, "Five tips for designing a great vehicle wrap" (3M Graphics)](https://www.signs101.com/threads/five-tips-for-designing-a-great-vehicle-wrap.89714)
- Compound curves force heating and stretching the vinyl, which "creates inaccuracies"; templates have angular distortion (snippet). — [signshop.com](https://www.signshop.com/?p=22854)
- Substance Painter 11.1: fonts on a Ribbon path deform along its curves; symmetric fill layers can flip an axis so mirrored logos stay readable. — [Adobe 11.1 release notes](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/release-notes/version-11-1)
- GT7: "aligned with surface" keeps a sticker's proportions as if physically applied; "aligned with camera" pre-distorts it so it reads unchanged from the application camera. — [Coach Dave Academy](https://coachdaveacademy.com/tutorials/how-to-use-the-gt7-livery-editor/)
- Forza: letters are built and grouped in the flat vinyl-group editor (grid) and placed as one group (snippet). — [Forza forums](https://forums.forza.net/t/are-there-any-tips-and-tricks-threads-for-creating-vinyl-groups-in-horizon-3/44990)
- GT7 requires text converted to paths in SVG decals (snippet). — [racinggames.gg](https://racinggames.gg/gran-turismo/how-to-upload-decals-to-gran-turismo-7/)
- Alpine kept its large "A" mark on one side rear panel and kept other areas clean for sponsor text. — [Motor Sport Magazine](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/f1/how-alpine-designed-its-striking-new-f1-livery/)
- Readability: high colour contrast keeps logos and numbers crisp (Popin); clarity of logos and no overcrowding (Blackmore). — [Popin Graphics](https://www.popingraphics.com/motorsport-livery/the-complete-guide-to-motorsport-livery-design-and-race-car-wraps); [Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/how-a-race-car-gets-its-paint-job-1752512826/)

### Inferences
- Lettering succeeds when its placement is chosen by flatness first and composition second; projecting text across a curved or multi-panel area is what fails. A measurable "flat enough for text" zone per panel is the practical gate.
- Two valid strategies: (1) text on a flat zone, projected along the zone's normal (surface-aligned, keeps proportions); (2) text deliberately following one chosen curve (ribbon), never both freely.

### Gaps
- No quantitative flatness/curvature threshold for legible text on cars was found.

## 6. AI in livery design, 2025-2026

### Takeaway
Text-to-livery exists for sim racing (Ailivery: chat brief, follow-up questions on colours/mood/numbers/sponsors, paints onto the sim's real template, exports DDS/TGA/zip) and for real wraps (Wrapmate, May 2026; WrapStudio AI). Ailivery publicly lists the same failures this project's user complains about: stripes crooked where two body pieces meet, a part painted the wrong way round, and multi-change requests only partly applied. Communities push back: Trading Paints discourages generative-AI paints and bans AI preview images as the first Showroom picture because they distort the cars.

### Cited Findings
- Ailivery workflow: Brief (pick car, set direction) -> Generate ("AI paints onto real template") -> Refine (chat, "draw edits right on the paint, place your logos, and rewind to any earlier version") -> Export (TGA/DDS); "every render is checked against the template, so the structure holds". — [Ailivery, AI race car livery generator](https://www.ai-livery.com/ai-race-car-livery-generator)
- Ailivery asks follow-up questions about "colours, mood, anything you're picturing"; can extract colours/logos from a pasted brand website; supports iRacing, Assetto Corsa, rFactor 2, Le Mans Ultimate, AMS2, Project Motor Racing, LFS, BeamNG, ETS2/ATS, MX Bikes, GP Bikes, Kart Racing Pro; exports "TGA, DDS or JPG skin, a mod ZIP, or the raw PNG". — [Ailivery home](https://www.ai-livery.com/)
- Ailivery's stated limitations: "A stripe can land crooked where two body pieces meet" (workarounds: "Make it safe", which keeps graphics within single pieces, or "I know Photoshop" for manual edge correction); "Now and then a part of the body is painted the wrong way round"; "A refinement is most accurate when you ask for a single change. Bundle several changes into one message and it often applies only some of them." — [Ailivery home](https://www.ai-livery.com/)
- Ailivery pricing conflicts between sources: its own page says "monthly plans from $9.99 a month" with a free tier — [Ailivery](https://www.ai-livery.com/ai-race-car-livery-generator); a review lists non-expiring credit packs ($9.99/200, $19.99/500, $39.99/1,200 credits) — [iRacerHUB review](https://iracerhub.com/ailivery-iracing-livery-generator/). Likely pricing changed; treat as subscription today.
- Reviewer verdict: output "clean and accurate to the templates"; won't match skilled designers on "fine detail or original concept". — [iRacerHUB](https://iracerhub.com/ailivery-iracing-livery-generator/)
- Trading Paints "prefers that content created with generative AI is not used or posted in the Trading Paints Showroom"; AI-made paints or previews are subject to moderation; AI scenes "tend to distort or take unrealistic liberties with how cars appear", so the first Showroom image should be an iRacing screenshot. — [Trading Paints help, "Is AI-generated content allowed?"](https://help.tradingpaints.com/showroom/is-ai-generated-content-allowed/); [Trading Paints, 2026-01-02](https://blog.tradingpaints.com/this-week-on-trading-paints-january-2-2026/)
- Wrapmate (2026-05-13): AI wrap designs in under 30 s, rendered in 3D on the exact vehicle, 12 free designs, installation by 3M-certified installers. — [Under30CEO](https://www.under30ceo.com/wrapmate-launches-industry-first-ai-platform-to-design-price-and-purchase-vehicle-wraps-online/)
- Adobe's generative AI in Substance is texture/background generation (Sampler, Stager) and excluded from the Steam perpetual edition. — [CGPress](https://cgpress.org/archives/adobe-introduces-generative-ai-in-substance-3d-apps.html); [Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/substance-3d-painter-discussions/how-do-steam-perpetual-licenses-work-exactly/td-p/15536613)

### Inferences
- What works in AI livery tools: a conversational brief with follow-up questions on mood; painting onto the real template; a check against the template; version rewind; drawing on the paint to point at changes. What fails: graphics crossing piece boundaries, part orientation, bundled edits. These failures match this project's (tapes with gaps/steps at seams; designs placed without judgement), suggesting they come from painting per piece/UV rather than from the AI's taste.
- Ailivery's "Make it safe" is a deliberate retreat (keep graphics inside one piece) rather than a fix; a tool that paints in 3D across pieces can do better, but only if crossings are handled by geometry, not hope.
- Community trust depends on previews that match the game; any AI-rendered or stylised preview is seen as misleading.

### Gaps
- No public information on which AI model Ailivery uses, whether it shows a 3D preview, or how it handles numbers/text on curves.
- No AI livery tool targeting Trackmania was found.
- No independent user reviews (Reddit/forums) on AI livery quality were retrievable.

## Applicability to this tool

Process lessons (how Claude should work with the user):
- **Brief like a pro, in taste terms only.** Blackmore and the interview practice start from what the client does *not* want and from references outside cars (music, painters) ([Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/how-a-race-car-gets-its-paint-job-1752512826/); [Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1125360_designing-a-race-car-livery-is-like-walking-on-a-tightrope)). One "what should it never look like?" question fits the rule "only ask about taste".
- **The brand book comes before the car, and is one page.** Pros design from a "design language/DNA" ([Mercedes](https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/news/insight-how-to-design-an-f1-car-livery); [Popin](https://www.popingraphics.com/motorsport-livery/the-complete-guide-to-motorsport-livery-design-and-race-car-wraps)). For this tool: palette with a finish per colour (gloss/matte/metal, as ACC gives each layer one material, [Bsimracing](https://bsimracing.com/?p=80816)), one motif, type (only if asked), where the car is quiet (negative space), how much detail and at what size, and the hero view. Shown in the Lab as a board the user picks from, then every layer obeys it.
- **Whole-car directions first, details last, in rounds.** Pros narrow many rough whole-car directions (60 to 30 to 10 to 1; three rounds: rough concepts, develop one, balance and details). Map: round 1 = a few rough takes in flat colour blocks only (no tapes, text, bolts), user picks; round 2 = develop the picked one; round 3 = details, a few at a time, shown close up. This matches the existing RULES ("details a few at a time") and the user's "design the car as a whole".
- **Design for the camera that sees the car.** Alpine designed "clean from the front, more intricate as the car rotates around the camera" ([Motor Sport Magazine](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/f1/how-alpine-designed-its-striking-new-f1-livery/)); Spindrift uses five views and "TV zones" ([Spindrift](https://www.spindriftmedia.com/insights/race-car-livery-design-guide)). For Trackmania, the hero views are the chase camera (rear three-quarter from above) and the garage view; the Lab could offer those camera presets, and a composition check could be "does the idea read from the chase camera?".
- **Whether to follow body lines is the concept's call.** Clean liveries use body lines, graphic ones may ignore them ([Motor Authority, snippet](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1125360_designing-a-race-car-livery-is-like-walking-on-a-tightrope)); speed comes from angled elements following contours ([Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/how-a-race-car-gets-its-paint-job-1752512826/)). The brand book should state which, consistent with RULES' "a line needs a reason in the idea".
- **One change per iteration.** Ailivery found bundled refinements are only partly applied ([Ailivery](https://www.ai-livery.com/)). Treat each Lab note/drawing as a separate change, applied and shown one by one.
- **Check against the game, not only the viewer.** Blackmore taped printed mock-ups on the real car; Trading Paints insists on in-sim screenshots because renders "distort" ([Trading Paints](https://help.tradingpaints.com/showroom/is-ai-generated-content-allowed/)). Use the user's F12 screenshots whenever they come, and keep the viewer's camera and lighting comparable to the game's.

Tooling lessons (what the machinery should do):
- **Tapes as paths on the 3D surface, not as per-part strokes.** The industry fix for gaps and steps is a path drawn once on the surface and a stroke/texture laid along it "without any cuts", crossing "gaps and object boundaries" (Substance Painter Filled path and Ribbon, 2025) ([Adobe 11.1](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/substance-3d-painter/using/release-notes/version-11-1)). For this tool: represent a tape as one continuous 3D curve on the mesh (taken whole from a model line or offset from it), and paint each texel by its 3D distance to that curve along the surface, so UV seams and part boundaries cannot create steps. Then a gate: sample the tape's width and centre along its length in 3D and refuse it if width varies or the centre jumps.
- **Decide where a graphic stops, explicitly.** GT7 exposes wrap angle/depth limits and a projection mode; Ailivery offers "keep within one piece" ([Coach Dave](https://coachdaveacademy.com/tutorials/how-to-use-the-gt7-livery-editor/); [Ailivery](https://www.ai-livery.com/)). For this tool: each graphic declares whether it crosses parts (on purpose) or stops at an edge; the check flags only crossings that were not declared, matching the RULES ("a check names an accident, never a design").
- **Text only on flat zones, as one unit.** Pros put text on the flattest panels and treat logos as movable overlays ([signs101, 3M tips, snippet](https://www.signs101.com/threads/five-tips-for-designing-a-great-vehicle-wrap.89714)); Forza builds lettering flat and places the group. For this tool: compute per-part flatness (normal spread or curvature from the mesh), offer text only in zones under a threshold, projected along that zone's mean normal (GT7's "aligned with surface"), and refuse elsewhere; text along a curve only as a deliberate ribbon on one model line. Show the candidate zones to the user in the Lab before placing words.
- **Layers by material.** Mirror ACC: base, graphics, details as separate layers, each with one finish written to Skin_R (roughness in red, metallic in green per the Nadeo pack). Keep colours away from pure black/white ([Bsimracing](https://bsimracing.com/?p=80816)) and avoid long subtle gradients on Skin_B, since BC1 is 5:6:5 and bands.
- **Relief details belong on Details.** Skin has no normal map ([Nadeo pack](https://devtrackers.gg/trackmania/p/3490d99c-stadium-car-ressources-all-you-need-to-create-skins-for-the-stadium-cars)); bolts or seams on the body can only be painted, so they read flat close up. Place the few meaningful fasteners where the Details set (with its normal map) already has them, or paint them knowing they are flat.
- **Symmetry with readable text.** Mirror graphics left/right by default, but flip text back so it reads (Painter 11.1 does this for fill layers).
- **Off-the-shelf options and costs.** Substance 3D Painter 2026 on Steam is $199.99 one time (no AI features, updates to March 2027) and has the best path tools; worth considering only if the tool needs a human-grade reference or a baking step, since it is a GUI app. Blender (free) with DECALmachine (paid add-on, price unverified) projects decals on curves and bakes to UVs, scriptable in Python, which fits a headless pipeline better. Ailivery (subscription from $9.99/month per its page) is the closest analogue and a useful benchmark of failure modes, not a component. TM Skin Studio (open source, three.js, JS DDS encoder) is worth reading for ideas but uses an approximate car model.
- **What changed recently and matters:** Painter's Ribbon path (Nov 2025) made text and tapes along surface paths a standard pro tool; F1 25 and FH6 moved to free placement on the surface; Wrapmate (May 2026) shows 3D-on-exact-vehicle AI design as mainstream for real wraps; Trading Paints set the norm that previews must be real in-game captures.
