# Sources

Both zips have been renamed. Their contents are unchanged: the original bytes, under the
hashes below.

## CarSport-Template.zip

- **From:** Nadeo, the studio that makes Trackmania.
- **Original name:** `Trackmania-Skin-Details-2021-02-18.zip`
- **Download:** https://nadeo-download.cdn.ubi.com/trackmania/website/resources/Trackmania-Skin-Details-2021-02-18.zip
  (linked from https://www.trackmania.com/media?lang=en)
- **Contains:** `ReadMe.txt`, Nadeo's instructions for the skin files; `UV_Skin.png`,
  `UV_Skin_Hollow.png`, `UV_Details.png` and `UV_Details_Hollow.png`, the flat UV maps.
- **sha256:** `d2ade95ad06f104cbe588126f27ef2ef6fd6f4aaf31620cd579c3b2a95a25a14`

## CarSport-Model.zip

- **From:** a community upload on Sketchfab. It is **not** a download from Nadeo.
- **Original name:** `trackmania-2020-carsport-2.zip`
- **Model page:** https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/trackmania-2020-carsport-c8b80bfc1ed1427eb37f3eba8d1ecfbf
- **Author:** amogusstrikesback2 (https://sketchfab.com/amogusstrikesback2)
- **Licence:** CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Reusing it means
  crediting the author.
- **Contains:**
  - `source/StadiumCAR2020_OffsetFix.zip`: the car as an FBX mesh, with its unpainted
    DDS textures.
  - `textures/*.png`: the same textures as PNG files.
- **sha256:** `9a08024c6a41cf75dedbf2c88ec0b9d00c770365a5924da6493610486bbfb6ff`
